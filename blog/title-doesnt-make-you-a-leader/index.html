A title doesn't make you a leader.

January 15, 2026

I've seen "Chief" titles who couldn't lead a team meeting. And I've watched individual contributors inspire entire companies.

The difference? True leaders operate differently:

They show up for their people in the moments that matter. They notice when someone's struggling. They ask how you're really doing.

They've done the work. They've written the code, created the designs, built the docs. But they're smart enough to step back and let their team own it now.

They grow other leaders, spotting potential in you before you see it yourself. Then they create the space for you to develop it.

They communicate relentlessly. Clear vision. Constant updates. Open door. No surprises.

Here's what I know after all these years in tech leadership: Communication and active involvement aren't just important. They're everything.

The best leaders I've worked with weren't always the ones with the biggest titles.

While my job titles haven't always been prestigious, I strive to be a strong leader in every role. I hope that in our interactions, I've demonstrated what a good leader should be.

What leadership qualities matter most to you?
